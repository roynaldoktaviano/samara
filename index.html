Good to know

Charter pricing starts from USD 14,000+ per night and can be tailored to each journey, depending on the chosen region, season, and duration.

Otium comfortably accommodates up to 12 guests, with flexible arrangements for two additional guests when required, including a separate twin bunk cabin suitable for children or entourage.

Komodo is typically at its best between April and November, while Raja Ampat is most favourable from October to April. We help guests select the ideal timing for their preferred itinerary.

Charters typically begin at five to seven nights. Journeys of ten to twelve nights offer a more comfortable pace, while fourteen nights or more allow truly immersive itineraries across multiple regions.

Otium is equipped with onboard Wi-Fi supported by Starlink, satellite communications, and mobile networks where available. Coverage may vary in remote regions — a natural opportunity to disconnect when desired.
