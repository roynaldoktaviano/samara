Marketing spend is everything: Meta and Google plus PR, influencers, content, events, agent trips and agency fees, each spread over the enquiries it could have brought (method in Sources, under "Leads with full marketing cost"). CPL and CAC divide it by every lead and booking in the selection. Results are grouped by the month the enquiry came in; the change is against the same period last year. Marketing-attributed value is bookings whose enquiry came through a marketing channel (not an agent, not direct or a referral). It is attributed, not proven incremental: what marketing caused on top of what would have happened anyway needs holdout or on/off tests.

        Phases

        • Phase 1 — reliable visibility. Replace imported files with live CRM, booking system, ad accounts and spend record; agree the open definitions; confirm owners.
        • Phase 2 — connected intelligence. Coverage, risk, timing and market opportunity running on real data; agency fields and guest ID added to the CRM.
        • Phase 3 — predictive intelligence. Replace trend forecasts and fixed risk points with models once there are two or more seasons of clean history; add anomaly detection and holdout-based incrementality.

        Where this build departs from the review

        • Attributed, not incremental. The dashboard labels marketing-attributed value plainly and does not claim incremental revenue: that needs holdout or on/off tests, which belong in Phase 3.
        • Fixed rules before models. Coverage score, risk index, forecasts and watch thresholds are transparent rules with their thresholds written down, as the review advised. They are starting points to tune on real history.
        • Overview stays a summary. The detailed tables live in their tabs; the overview carries headline numbers, the forward look and Performance Watch.
        • Observations, not instructions. Performance Watch states what moved and where to look. Turning findings into tasks stays in the Command Center.
        • What the imported files can't show yet. Per-agent results, website coverage, holds and blocked inventory, negotiation stage, lifetime value across brands and digital infrastructure spend are listed above with the data each needs, rather than shown with made-up numbers.
        • Eleven modules, four groups. Navigation groups the modules under Commercial, Marketing performance, Customer intelligence and Planning, plus this governance tab.

          Inventory risk index and coverage score

          Coverage is five checks worth 20 each, so a score says exactly what is missing: a paid campaign aimed at the departure; the destination in live or planned press, influencer or content work; ads bought in one of the two countries that book this destination most; an agent fam trip or trade show for it in the last 12 months or next 3; and enough qualified enquiries in the last 60 days to reach the usual fill. Website presence is not tracked yet. Risk is fixed points out of 10 (days away, share unsold, pace, booking window, warm conversations, coverage, recent bookings): 6+ high, 3–5 medium.
